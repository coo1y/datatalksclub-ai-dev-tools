2. What are the 2-4 features your spec settled on?
**Weekly planning session** — the group opens the app together and defines the week's chore list, assigning each chore to a person.
**Daily use** — anyone can open the app, log in with their PIN, and check off chores as they complete them.
**Missed chores** — if a chore isn't checked off, it simply stays visibly undone. No punishment, no auto-reassignment.
**History review** — anyone can look back at past weeks to see who completed or missed which chores.

3. Which file do you edit to include your app in the Django project?
`chore_tracker/settings.py` — you add the app to the INSTALLED_APPS list. That's what I did
  earlier when registering chores

Milestone 1 — Data Model
